# Lumen Bath guide

How to play a bath, shape the room, and share the view you are looking at. The same steps are in the app. Press the **i** on the canvas to walk them. The **i** beside a slider or switch explains that one control. While the tour is open, every one of those notes is showing.

Headphones help. Left and right are two different horns, not a pan knob.

## Play

Press play, or the space bar, and the bowls ring. Space again pauses. If you paused on purpose, it stays paused while you look around the interface. The play button pulses so you can see it is waiting.

A first visit opens a preset or a fan bath at random.

## The room

Click the floor and Session opens down the left side. Click empty air, or the X, and it hides again.

**Room size** grows or shrinks the floor. Bowls and ears can be dragged anywhere on the new floor, out to the walls. The room will not shrink inside them, and they stop when they meet.

**Shapes** are Rotunda, Cube, Shoebox (the classic hall), Corridor, Chapel, Nave, Dome, Fan hall, Cave, Courtyard, Golden hall, Golden chapel, Octagon, Apse, and Ellipse. Golden hall and Golden chapel are golden rectangles. Bowls keep their size when the shape changes. A shape sets the room color of the sound. You can still move each of these after:

| Control | What you hear |
|---|---|
| Decay | How long the room keeps ringing after the glass. |
| Early reflections | The first bounce off the nearest walls. |
| Diffusion | The tail breaks up into a wash. |
| Absorption | Soft walls swallow the highs. |
| Flutter | A rapid echo between parallel walls. |
| Standing waves | Bass the room itself reinforces. |
| Air loss | Far bowls lose their edge. |
| Slapback | One distinct echo, then quiet. |
| Bass bloom | Low end gathers in the corners. |
| Envelopment | How far the tail wraps around you. |

**Phrase** is how the bath breathes. Sustain holds. Breath, tide, mallet, and canon give it a cycle, and the length slider sets that cycle.

**Veil** is a quiet detuned twin on every bowl, so the beats do not lock. **Crown shimmer** adds the high glass.

**Glass hall** sits behind the dry crystal, which stays full. Hall, hall size, and silk shape that tail. **Transpose** shifts every bowl by cents.

## Bowls

Click a bowl. A box wraps it. The side menu lists its glass, pitch, and level.

- Gold corners resize it. It stops before it would pass through another bowl or an ear.
- Bottom points lengthen the wall while the rim stays. Those points hide when the bowl is already on the floor. Drag the bowl itself downward to grow it until it meets the floor, another bowl, or an ear. Nothing draws through the floor.
- On a phone, the sliders button opens size and place before the full settings.
- Shift-click adds more, then move or scale them together. Command-Z puts them back.
- The menu on the selection has an X to remove it and a copy mark. A copy appears beside it.

Glass types are clear quartz, frosted crystal, gold, platinum, rose, obsidian, aqua, emerald, phantom, and selenite. Each colors the partials.

## Domes

The shell button hangs a dome face-down over the glass. Waves that rise into the opening come back down, and the horns hear that return. A wider shell catches more of the room. The top of the scale matches the hall floor. From outside the shell it stays nearly clear, so you can still see the bowls. Move the view underneath and it fills in.

- Drag the crown to set the height. Higher sends the bounce back later.
- Drag the point on the rim to scale it.
- Drag the shell to slide it.
- Material, pitch, reflection, spread, and brightness change the bounce.
- Select it and the icons remove or copy it. Four is the ceiling.

Turn on waves and the return shows as a shell in a mix of the bowl color and the dome color.

Five presets show the useful cases:

| Preset | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Hall Canopy | One shell as wide as the hall. Everything that rises comes back over the middle. |
| Note Cup | A tight bright cup over one bowl, tuned to that note. |
| Split Return | Two domes a fifth apart. The left horn takes the low shell, the right horn the high one. |
| Late Ceiling | A dark shell at the top of the hall, so the bounce arrives late. |
| Soft Frost | A frosted canopy that spreads the return instead of focusing it. |

## Ears and stems

Each ear is two hollow horns, labeled Left and Right. The wide mouth faces the sound. The horns stay put until you pull a ball.

- A red circle sits outside each cone, on the ear and on every stem, so the size is easy to see. The red ball on that circle only changes size. Pull it out and that cone gets larger. Push it in and it gets smaller.
- A ball on the back of each horn lifts just that one. The long lever behind the head lifts both together.
- The ball past the mouth aims that horn. Pull it up or down and the opening follows, all the way to straight up or straight down.
- A ring around each horn turns that horn on its own. The ring above the head turns the whole ear.
- A corner of the box turns the ear. An edge sizes both horns.
- Drag the head up to four times the old ceiling.
- Nothing here will go through the floor, a wall, a bowl, or the other horn.

A wider mouth, a higher volume, and an opening aimed at a bowl gather more of that bowl. Waves that reach the mouth, including bounces off walls and returns off domes, are what that horn collects. That is what you hear. Height and angle change the hit, because the sound is spatial.

The second **+** adds an ear for recording. You hear the main ear until Cycle through reaches a stem, or you make that stem the ear. In the ear tab the list is the order: the first row is the ear you hear, and the rows under it are stems.

Select a stem and press the ear icon. It becomes what you hear, and the old ear turns into a stem. To turn the ear into a stem by hand, press the stem icon, then select another ear. You hear that one as a preview, mixed with the current ear. Either way the handoff is a short fade, so the sound does not jump or go quiet.

**Cycle through** walks that list. The slider runs from 0.25 seconds to 33.33 seconds. You hear one stem, then a short fade into the next, with no gap. The other stems stay quiet until the cycle reaches them. They are not all played at once in stereo.

A download with more than one ear keeps each extra ear as its own left and right, including height. Presets that use every stem are marked for download. A live stream stays stereo.

## Waves

The wave button draws every front in the room, not as a flat ring on the floor. Each one is a shell of three rings, so you can see it from the side. It grows out of that bowl in the bowl’s color.

- A bounce off a wall keeps the color.
- A dome sends the rising shell back down across the opening, in a mix of the bowl and the dome. That return is part of what the horns collect.
- Where two shells meet, the spark mixes the two colors.
- A shell lights a horn when it reaches the mouth.

Click the wave button again to see the fronts in three dimensions.

## Cast

Cast pairs this screen with another, by a code or a QR code.

The screen with the larger view drops its menus and shows the features you turn on from the smaller one. It draws in ultra HD when the machine can. The smaller screen is the controller.

After you pair, the smaller screen lists every bowl, the ear, and each stem as a button in its own color, with its icon and name. Pick a bowl and slide a finger around the rim. The larger screen sings that rim in real time. Tap the bowl to strike it, like a mallet. Five mallets sit under that view. Hold the bowl to keep one mallet for this session. Saving the template keeps that mallet with the bowl.

Record on the mobile device and the file is saved in both places. The desktop save is a browser download, with no extra prompt.

## Presets

Presets and Fan Created sit on the second line of the header.

Open Presets and tap a tag to keep one set. Sort by:

- **New**, the latest set first, then the weekly AI baths
- **Features**, grouped by what the bath uses
- **Loved**, from thumbs, plays, and saves
- **Played**
- **Name**

Thumbs at the bottom corners step through the presets. Down is the previous, up is the next. Each thumb is kept, so later sets can follow what people actually leave up. Move more than one bowl or ear and the thumbs tuck away. The small thumb on the left brings them back.

Baths marked AI generated use aim, mallets, height, and cycles. The week’s draft is written with Venice when `VENICE_API_KEY` is set.

## Fan Created

Baths people saved. Names are usernames, never an email. A first visit may open one of these at random.

Open any of them and change it in the room. Saving makes a new one. A bath you saved can be renamed, or updated in place, once you are using that bath and have changed it.

Each card’s icon is the snapshot taken when the bath was shared or saved. That same picture is the link preview.

## Share

Share, at the bottom right of the desktop view, has a label and an icon.

1. It takes a picture of the scene, from the camera angle you are on.
2. It stores the bowls, domes, ears, room, waves, Cycle through, and whether playback was on.
3. You name it. You have to be signed in. The address is your name and that title, `/s/<name>/<title>.html`.
4. Anyone who opens the link gets that scene and that camera. If it was playing, the first click starts the sound. Browsers will not start audio on their own.

The picture in the prompt is the Open Graph card and the Fan Created icon. Update the link, or update a bath you own, and a fresh snapshot replaces both.

Templates that ship with the app already have links under `/s/lumen/<id>.html`. This view, Templates, and Saved are the three tabs in the share sheet. Copy, the system share sheet, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit, LinkedIn, email, messages, embed, and save-card all use the link you just made.

Signed-in saves, your name and bio, and each weekly AI note are also copied to the `library` branch on GitHub, so they are not only in this browser.

## Listening mode

Leave the screen alone.

- After a few seconds the menus, boxes, and labels fade and the view slowly turns.
- After three minutes the room, the floor, and the ears fade, and only the bowls keep spinning.
- After thirteen minutes the bowls fade too, and only the wave shells remain.

Move, click, or press a key and the room comes back.

## Record

Record writes a file. One ear is stereo. Extra ears are stems, each with its own left and right cones, including height. Cycle through keeps walking the ear while you listen.

## Bugs and features

On the left of the bath, the bug button and the spark button open a note. Type the letters in the picture, then send it. The note is filed as an issue on [maximusmaximus/lumen-bath](https://github.com/maximusmaximus/lumen-bath). Venice reads it. If it looks like a small, safe change, a draft pull request is opened and left for review. It does not merge by itself. A note for the owner stays in Dash, under Admin, and is not put on the public issue.
